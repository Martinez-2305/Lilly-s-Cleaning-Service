Reliable Cleaning Solutions – Brand Guidelines
1. Brand Overview

Reliable Cleaning Solutions positions itself as a premium, trustworthy, and detail-focused cleaning service. The visual identity reflects professionalism, safety, and high standards, with a subtle “royal” touch to reinforce quality.

Brand Values:

Reliability
Attention to detail
Professionalism
High-end service
Trust & safety
2. Logo Usage
Primary Logo

The primary logo consists of:

Shield emblem (symbolizing protection & trust)
Crown (premium quality)
House icon (residential focus)
Sparkle elements (cleanliness)
Company name + tagline
Clear Space

Always maintain spacing around the logo equal to the height of the crown symbol.
No text or elements should enter this space.

Minimum Size
Digital: 120px width minimum
Print: 30mm width minimum
Incorrect Usage

Do NOT:

Stretch or distort the logo
Change colors outside brand palette
Remove elements (crown/shield/sparkles)
Place on low-contrast backgrounds
3. Color Palette

Your color palette is strong already—keep it tight and consistent.

Primary Colors
Deep Navy Blue (#0F2E3D approx)
→ Backgrounds, authority, trust
Royal Blue Gradient (#1C4E80 → #2FA4FF)
→ Logo highlights, accents
Secondary Colors
Light Blue (#5BC0EB)
→ Accents, highlights, CTAs
White (#FFFFFF)
→ Text, contrast, cleanliness
Usage Ratio
70% Dark Blue
20% Light/Accent Blue
10% White
4. Typography
Primary Font (Headings)
Modern Sans Serif (e.g. Montserrat / Poppins / Lato)
Bold, uppercase for authority
Secondary Font (Body Text)
Clean sans-serif (e.g. Open Sans / Roboto)
Regular or light weight
Tagline Style
Slightly lighter weight
Italic or softer tone to contrast main title
5. Logo Variations

To keep things flexible across platforms:

Full Logo
Icon + text + tagline
Use for website, banners, social headers
Compact Logo
Icon + company name only
Use for uniforms, vans, invoices
Icon Only
Shield emblem
Use for:
Social media profile picture
App icon
Watermark
6. Imagery Style

Your visuals should reinforce cleanliness + professionalism.

Do Use:
Bright, well-lit interiors
Before/after cleaning shots
Close-ups of sparkling surfaces
Clean uniforms & equipment
Avoid:
Dark or cluttered imagery
Low-quality stock photos
Overly staged or fake-looking scenes
7. Tone of Voice

Keep your messaging aligned with the premium feel.

Voice Characteristics
Confident, not pushy
Professional, but friendly
Clear and benefit-driven
Examples

Instead of:
“Cheap cleaning services available”

Use:
“Professional cleaning you can rely on, every time.”

8. Tagline Usage

Primary tagline:

Beyond cleanliness. Beyond expectations.

Use:

On website headers
Marketing materials
Social banners

Avoid overusing it in small spaces (like invoices or footers).

9. Applications
Where this brand should shine:
Website (clean, minimal layout)
Van signage (high contrast)
Staff uniforms (dark base + white logo)
Business cards (matte navy + gloss logo)
Social media branding
10. Brand Feel (Summary)

If someone sees your brand, they should immediately think:

“These guys are professional”
“They’re not cheap—but they’re worth it”
“I can trust them in my home”
